How it works

Four steps, and nobody has to leave the school gate.

1List it

Post the item in about a minute. Choose to sell, swap or give it away.

2Chat

Message the seller or buyer inside the app to agree the details.

3Meet at school

Pick it up at the school gate. No delivery, no strangers.

4Pay or swap

Pay with M-Pesa, trade for something your child needs, or claim it free.

Questions

Is this live yet?

Not quite. It is a working prototype, so M-Pesa payments and seller replies in the demo are simulated. Real payments come with launch.

Which schools can use it?

We are starting with one school, then adding more. Pick your school at the top of the app.

Is it safe?

Everyone is from the same school community, sellers can be school-verified, and every handover happens at school.

What can I list?

Uniforms, textbooks, shoes, stationery and sports kit. Sell it, swap it, or give it away for free.

How does StudySwap make money?

A small fee on each sale, optional boosts to show your item first, term-start bundles, and StudySwap Plus.
